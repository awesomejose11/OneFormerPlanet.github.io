What is A Big Open Cosmos?

A Big Open Cosmos, abbreviated as ABOC, is an indie animated YouTube series. It is heavily ispired by the PlanetBalls Community. The show is aiming for a comediec side of genre, while also maintaing great direction.

The Creator

The Person behind the series is a person known as OneFormerPlanet, also behind the creator of CandyBalls. With CandyBalls being on hiatus for the summer, we can take this time to develop a new project with the same passion.

Why Support Now?

The goal of ABOC is to make it seperate from the team of CandyBalls. Basically, there will be 2 different teams for each individual project, while making sure we are able to animate and keep both shows consistent with uploads, even with more important things in our life coming up. ABOC has been planned since November 2025, and the best part, the first 5 episodes are just in production. For now, working for the show is a hobby, but soon we would like to compensate people who help us with production, which is why we need you! Any general support is good, wether your sharing it to a friend who might be intrested, promoting the project, or simply just subscribing, anything would help launch the show they way we are heading!

Drawbacks

Although it doesn't look to hard to make, do remember we have lives in the outside world, you also might be wondering: Why only one owner? What if something happens? We will be looking for people to hire if the first episode does succeed, meaning that we will try our best not to be transparent about anything that happens.
